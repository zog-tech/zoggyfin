Zoggyfin is a fast, calm client for your own Jellyfin media server. Sign in to your server and play your movies, shows and music on your iPhone and iPad.

You need a Jellyfin server to use Zoggyfin. Zoggyfin has no media of its own: it plays only what is on the servers that you add. To try the app first, use the public Jellyfin demo server.

VIDEO
• Video player with Picture in Picture and AirPlay
• Audio and subtitle tracks, quality and playback speed
• Scrubbing with preview images and chapter marks
• Skip Intro, Skip Credits and a next-episode countdown
• Resume where you stopped; progress syncs with your server

MUSIC AND AUDIOBOOKS
• Background playback with controls on the lock screen and in Control Center
• Synced lyrics when your server has them
• A queue you can reorder, plus shuffle and repeat
• Instant Mix from a song, album, artist or genre
• Audiobook mode with speed control, chapters and a sleep timer

DOWNLOADS YOU CAN SEE
• Download movies, episodes, seasons, albums and playlists
• See the queue, the progress and the space used
• Downloads continue in the background
• Choose the original file or a smaller copy
• Play offline; your progress goes to the server later

WATCH TOGETHER
• SyncPlay groups with other users of your server
• Nearby: watch or listen in sync with people near you on the same Wi-Fi. Bluetooth helps nearby devices find each other.

MADE FOR YOUR LIBRARY
• Home with Continue Watching, Next Up and Recently Added
• Libraries with sort and filters, collections and playlists
• Add to a playlist in two taps
• Fast search with results grouped by type
• Every item has a picture, even without server artwork
• More than one server and user, with home and internet addresses
• An optional Wi-Fi rule picks the home address of your server on your home Wi-Fi
• Quick Connect sign-in, light and dark appearance
• A wide layout for iPad

FOR SERVER ADMINISTRATORS
• Invite a person with a link or a QR code
• An admin panel: sessions, users, libraries, scheduled tasks and logs

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Zoggyfin has no account of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks only to the servers that you add.

Zoggyfin is open source under the MPL-2.0. It is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project. Jellyfin is free software for your own media server.
